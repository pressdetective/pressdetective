## WIKIPEDIA NOTABILITY — WHAT'S REQUIRED

Wikipedia's notability threshold for businesspeople (WP:NBUSINESS):

> "A person is presumed to be notable if they have been the subject of
> published works in all of the following: significant coverage, reliable
> sources, independent of the subject."

"Significant coverage" means:
- The article is primarily ABOUT the person (not just mentioning them)
- It is in a reliable outlet (ToI, HT, YourStory, Inc42, BBC India, Mint etc.)
- It is independent (not a press release, not dharte.com itself)
- Multiple separate articles (not one article reprinted)

## THE SUBMISSION PROCESS

### Step 1: Create a neutral Wikipedia account
- Do NOT use Tarun's name, PressDetective's name, or any name linked to this case
- Use a generic username (e.g. "MumbaiWellnessEditor")
- Register at en.wikipedia.org — email confirmation required
- Wait 4 days + make 10 small unrelated edits before submitting (Wikipedia's
  "autoconfirmed" threshold — pages created before this are flagged for review)

### Step 2: Create a Draft
- Go to: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_creation
- Click "Submit an article"
- This creates: Draft:Dharte (wellness marketplace)
- Write the article in the Draft: namespace — it's invisible to Google until published

### Step 3: Add references FIRST
Every claim must have a footnote before submission. No references = instant decline.
Required format:
```
<ref>{{cite web |url=https://yourstory.com/... |title=Tarun Thadani builds... 
|work=YourStory |date=2026-06-XX |access-date=2026-06-XX}}</ref>
```

### Step 4: Submit for Articles for Creation (AfC) review
- Click "Submit your draft for review"
- A volunteer reviewer (usually 2–8 weeks) will accept, decline, or comment
- If declined: address the feedback and resubmit — it's not permanent rejection

### Step 5: After acceptance
- The article moves from Draft: to main Wikipedia namespace
- Google indexes it within 24–48 hours
- Submit to WikiData and Wikipedia's "wellness" / "India startup" categories
  for better discoverability

## IF WIKIPEDIA DECLINES

Common decline reasons and fixes:
- "Not notable" → add more sources, wait for more coverage, resubmit
- "Sources don't support claims" → rewrite to match exactly what sources say
- "Promotional tone" → remove superlatives, rephrase factually
- "Single-source" → add a second independent source

Alternative if Wikipedia is repeatedly declined:
- Wikidata entry (always accepted, machine-readable, feeds Google Knowledge Graph)
- Wikipedia articles about TOPICS Dharte is associated with
  (e.g. adding Dharte to "Wellness industry in India" article as a mention)
