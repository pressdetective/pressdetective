Subject: Complaint of abuse of process / threat to personal liberty — fabricated non-bailable FIR No. 0654/2022 (Dadar PS, Mumbai)

To the Secretary / Registrar, National Human Rights Commission, India

Respected Sir/Madam,

I respectfully lodge this complaint seeking intervention of this Commission regarding a fabricated non-bailable FIR that threatens the personal liberty of two innocent persons, in violation of Article 21 of the Indian Constitution.

FACTS:
1. On or about 2 June 2022, at a private restaurant gathering in Worli, Mumbai, two guests — Mr. Ali Asgar Merchant and Mr. Abhishek Badriprasad Saraf — argued and Mr. Saraf was slapped by Mr. Merchant. This is at most a Section 323 IPC offence (bailable, compoundable).

2. Mr. Tarun Thadani (founder, Dharte/dharte.com) had only sent the invitations to this gathering; he was NOT present at the time of the argument.

3. On 4 June 2022, Mr. Saraf himself filed an online complaint (ID 23244/2022). In his own words at that time, it alleged ONLY the slap — there was NO mention of extortion, NO demand for Rs 1 crore, and NO mention of Mr. Thadani.

4. Two months later, on 13 August 2022, FIR No. 0654/2022 was registered (Dadar Police Station, routed through CB-CID Anti-Extortion Cell) alleging a Rs 1 crore "extortion" and naming Mr. Thadani for the first time — despite:
   - No accused being examined before registration
   - No call detail records verified
   - No bank statements verified
   - No CCTV footage verified

This transformation of a minor assault complaint into a non-bailable extortion case — two months later, with no new evidence — constitutes clear abuse of process and violation of the right to liberty of both Mr. Merchant and Mr. Thadani.

RELIEF SOUGHT:
(a) Call for a detailed report from the State of Maharashtra / Mumbai Police on the registration of FIR 0654/2022;

(b) Direct investigation into the documented irregularities and the complainant's conduct in altering his complaint two months after filing;

(c) Protect the liberty of the two persons by directing appropriate remedial measures (quashing or reinvestigation);

(d) Ensure that the authorities take lawful action against anyone found to have abused the criminal process.

I undertake to furnish all documents and evidence, and to depose before the Commission if required. This matter is sub-judice; I make no allegation of guilt against any person. I only seek that the documented irregularities receive the scrutiny and investigation they warrant.
