Subject: Formal complaint regarding false and fabricated FIR No. 0654/2022 (Dadar PS) and request for scrutiny and investigation into false complaint by Mr. Abhishek Saraf

Respected Sir/Madam,

I lodge this formal complaint regarding FIR No. 0654/2022, Dadar Police Station, registered under the CB-CID Anti-Extortion Cell on 13 August 2022, against Mr. Tarun Thadani (founder, Dharte/dharte.com) and Mr. Ali Asgar Merchant.

FACTS:
1. On or about 2 June 2022, at a restaurant in Worli, Mumbai, a private gathering was held. Mr. Tarun Thadani had only sent out the invitations; he was not present at the time of the argument that occurred.

2. During the gathering two guests — Mr. Ali Asgar Merchant and Mr. Abhishek Badriprasad Saraf — argued, and Mr. Saraf was slapped. At its highest this is an offence under Section 323 IPC (bailable).

3. On 4 June 2022, Mr. Saraf himself lodged an online complaint (ID 23244/2022). In his own words it alleged only the slap — no extortion, no demand of Rs 1 crore, and no mention of Mr. Thadani.

4. Approximately two months later, FIR No. 0654/2022 was registered (Dadar Police Station, 13 August 2022) and routed through the CB-CID Anti-Extortion Cell, now alleging — for the first time — a Rs 1 crore "extortion" and naming Mr. Thadani, who was not present. No accused was examined before registration; no call detail records, bank statements or CCTV — none of which support any extortion — were verified.

ALLEGATIONS:
The regular police having earlier not registered a serious case on so minor a matter, the complainant manufactured a false extortion narrative and caused a non-baille FIR to be registered through the Anti-Extortion Cell — falsely implicating an innocent man (Mr. Thadani) who had only sent invitations and was not present during the argument, and inflating a bailable slap into a charge carrying up to ten years.

REQUEST:
(1) Register and investigate the false / altered complaint by Mr. Saraf (the contradiction between ID 23244/2022 [assault only] and the later extortion version), under Sections 217/248 BNS (formerly IPC 182/211);

(2) Reinvestigate FIR 0654/2022 against the original complaint (ID 23244/2022) and verify by CDR/bank/CCTV whether any extortion was made out;

(3) Take lawful action after due investigation.

I make this complaint in good faith. I am willing to depose and to furnish the documents in my possession. This matter is sub-judice; nothing herein prejudges any pending proceeding.
