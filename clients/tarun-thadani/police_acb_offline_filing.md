         due-diligence verification of the complainant's antecedents.

Respected Sir/Madam,

1. On or about 2 June 2022, at a restaurant in Worli, Mumbai, a private gathering was
   held. Mr. Tarun Thadani, founder of Dharte (dharte.com), had only sent out the
   invitations; he was not present at the time of the argument that occurred.

2. During the gathering two guests — Mr. Ali Asgar Merchant and Mr. Abhishek Badriprasad
   Saraf — argued, and Mr. Saraf was slapped. At its highest this is an offence under
   Section 323 IPC (bailable).

3. On 4 June 2022, Mr. Saraf himself lodged an online complaint (ID 23244/2022). In his
   own words it alleged only the slap — no extortion, no demand of Rs 1 crore, and no
   mention of Mr. Thadani.

4. Approximately two months later, FIR No. 0654/2022 was registered (Dadar Police
   Station, 13 August 2022) and routed through the CB-CID Anti-Extortion Cell, now
   alleging — for the first time — a Rs 1 crore "extortion" and naming Mr. Thadani, who
   was not present. No accused was examined before registration; no call detail records,
   bank statements or CCTV — none of which support any extortion — were verified.

5. I therefore request the Bureau to: (a) investigate how a complaint that originally
   contained no extortion was converted, two months later, into a non-bailable extortion
   FIR; (b) examine whether the registration was procured by any corrupt or improper
   means; (c) verify the antecedents of the complainant, Mr. Abhishek Badriprasad Saraf
   (+91 98201 80065; 3rd Floor, Esplanade House, 29 Hazarimal Somani Marg, Fort, Mumbai
   400001; public record: party to Calcutta High Court proceedings CS 313/2012, Martin
   Burn Ltd); and (d) inquire into the circumstances of the one-sided press coverage of
   this case, including whether any improper inducement was involved — on which I make
   no allegation of fact and leave the determination to the Bureau.

I make this complaint in good faith. I am willing to depose and to furnish the documents
in my possession. This matter is sub-judice; nothing herein prejudges any pending
proceeding.
