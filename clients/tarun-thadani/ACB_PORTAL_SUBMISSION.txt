Subject: Complaint regarding FIR No. 0654/2022 (Dadar PS / CB-CID Anti-Extortion Cell) - Investigation into false/altered complaint registration and possible improper influence

Details:

Complaint by Santosh Sakpal (Independent Investigator, +91 82689 17276, santoshsakpal03@gmail.com) re FIR No. 0654/2022, Dadar Police Station, Mumbai, registered under CB-CID Anti-Extortion Cell.

FACTS:
1. On or about 2 June 2022, at a restaurant in Worli, Mumbai, a private gathering was held. Mr. Tarun Thadani, founder of Dharte (dharte.com), had only sent out the invitations; he was not present at the time of the argument that occurred.

2. During the gathering two guests — Mr. Ali Asgar Merchant and Mr. Abhishek Badriprasad Saraf — argued, and Mr. Saraf was slapped. At its highest this is an offence under Section 323 IPC (bailable).

3. On 4 June 2022, Mr. Saraf himself lodged an online complaint (ID 23244/2022). In his own words it alleged only the slap — no extortion, no demand of Rs 1 crore, and no mention of Mr. Thadani.

4. Approximately two months later, FIR No. 0654/2022 was registered (Dadar Police Station, 13 August 2022) and routed through the CB-CID Anti-Extortion Cell, now alleging — for the first time — a Rs 1 crore "extortion" and naming Mr. Thadani, who was not present. No accused was examined before registration; no call detail records, bank statements or CCTV — none of which support any extortion — were verified.

REQUEST FOR INVESTIGATION:
(a) Investigate how a complaint that originally contained no extortion was converted, two months later, into a non-bailable extortion FIR;

(b) Examine whether the registration was procured by any corrupt or improper means;

(c) Verify the antecedents of the complainant, Mr. Abhishek Badriprasad Saraf (+91 98201 80065; 3rd Floor, Esplanade House, 29 Hazarimal Somani Marg, Fort, Mumbai 400001; public record: party to Calcutta High Court proceedings CS 313/2012, Martin Burn Ltd);

(d) Inquire into the circumstances of the one-sided press coverage of this case, including whether any improper inducement was involved — on which I make no allegation of fact and leave the determination to the Bureau.

I make this complaint in good faith. I am willing to depose and to furnish the documents in my possession. This matter is sub-judice; nothing herein prejudges any pending proceeding.
