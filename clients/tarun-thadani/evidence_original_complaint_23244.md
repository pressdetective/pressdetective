# EVIDENCE — the complainant's OWN original complaint (the smoking gun)
*Recorded 21 June 2026 from documents provided by the client. Originals held; this file
captures the exculpatory substance for the case record. The originals contain third
parties' personal data and are NOT reproduced in full here.*

## 1. Original Online Complaint — Greater Mumbai Police, **Worli PS, Complaint Id 23244/2022**
- **Filed:** 04/06/2022 03:43:44 (computer-generated, "no signature required").
- **Complainant:** Abhishek Saraf (contact + email on the original; email is the **no-contact**
  address — never to be emailed).
- **In the complainant's own words (verbatim key phrases):**
  - he was at the Worli restaurant "**on an invite [from] tarun thadani**" — i.e. Mr. Thadani's
    role is the person who INVITED him.
  - "**ali assaulted me by hitting on the face and fell down**" — the assault is attributed
    solely to **Ali Asgar Merchant**.
  - "all recordings are available with the restaurant and on ali['s] phone."
- **What it does NOT contain:** NO extortion, NO demand of Rs 1 crore, and NO allegation that
  Mr. Thadani did anything beyond inviting the guests. The incident is dated ~12:30 pm on
  03/06/2022 at the Worli restaurant opening.
- **Significance:** this is the complainant's FIRST, contemporaneous account. It establishes an
  **assault only** (at most IPC 323, bailable). The Rs 1 crore "extortion" and Mr. Thadani's
  alleged involvement — the basis of FIR 0654/2022 (Dadar PS, 13 Aug 2022) — are ABSENT from it
  and surfaced ~2 months later. The FIR is therefore a materially altered, later version.
